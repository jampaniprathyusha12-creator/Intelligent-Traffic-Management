01 / UNDERSTANDING THE CHALLENGE

The problem behind the traffic.

Traffic signals must divide limited green-light time between roads with different traffic demands.

Every second of green time matters.
01

What is the problem?

Different roads have different numbers of waiting vehicles. However, the total available green-light time is limited. We need to decide how to distribute that time among the roads.

02

Why does it matter?

Poor allocation of signal time can leave some roads waiting longer than necessary. A better allocation can help serve more vehicles within the available time.

03

What does our program calculate?

It compares four algorithmic approaches to find or estimate a green-time allocation that serves as many vehicles as possible within a fixed time budget.

Project scope: This is an educational simulation using sample traffic data. It does not control real traffic signals or use live traffic information.
